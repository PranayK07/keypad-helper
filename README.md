Turn a three-key keypad and dial into a companion for your day. Switch profiles from the menu bar, settle into the warm coffee-shop interface, and keep your favorite controls close by.

| Your corner | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| ☕ **Coding** | Dictation, sending prompts, and reasoning controls in Codex. |
| 🎧 **Music** | Spotify play/pause, track skipping, and volume. |
| 🎙️ **Calls** | Microphone and deafen/camera shortcuts for Discord, Zoom, and Teams. |

## Pull up a chair

You'll need an **Apple Silicon Mac running macOS 13 or later** and a **Bright Data three-key, one-dial keypad**. Other keypad models haven't been tested.

1. **Download and unzip** the app above, then move **Keypad Companion** into Applications.
2. **New keypad?** Follow the [one-time setup](docs/first-time-setup.md) to prepare its buttons. The guide also covers the shortcuts needed for Coding.
3. **Open the app** and follow its setup prompts to enable Accessibility and Input Monitoring. Spotify may ask for Automation permission when you first use Music.
4. **Pick your profile** from the little symbol in the menu bar. You're ready to go.

This is an early release and isn't notarized by Apple yet. If macOS blocks it, see [Apple's guidance on opening downloaded apps](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445).

## Make yourself comfortable

- **Click the menu-bar symbol** to choose a profile. Right-click to cycle quickly.
- **The dial stays useful**—it controls the profile you're in, never switches profiles.
- **Settings → Overview** shows your controls and lets you pause them.
- **Settings → Profiles** lets you customize a profile without switching into it. Changes save automatically.
- **Settings → Preferences** handles launch at login, call-app selection, and volume steps.
- **Close settings** when you're done. The menu-bar companion keeps running.

Calls can follow whichever supported call app is in front, with Discord as the fallback. The profile itself stays where you put it.
